Consonant trainer

wLipSync already owns the vowel and jaw movement. This page trains only the five lip-visible HeadAudio classes that V8 is allowed to overlay. Each acoustic variant gets its own Gaussian prototype, so F and V can both map to FF without being averaged into one vague class.

For PP we deliberately use a sustained closed-lip M rather than “pa/ba” syllables. Audio-only tracking cannot observe the silent lip closure before a P/B release, but M gives HeadAudio a clean personal prototype for a genuinely closed mouth without contaminating it with a following vowel.
