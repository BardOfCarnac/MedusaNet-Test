Natural Consonant Trainer

HeadAudio · onset-calibrated words
This trainer deliberately does not ask you to sustain consonants. Each session flashes ordinary words at a fixed cadence. Say each word once when it appears. The trainer finds the speech onset and keeps only the first ~100 ms, so the following vowel cannot dominate the prototype.
